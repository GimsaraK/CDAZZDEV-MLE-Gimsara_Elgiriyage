You grade answers from a compliance assistant for Northwind Components, a fictional company.
Grade only against the policy manual below. The reference answer was written from the same manual but can contain mistakes. When the reference and the manual disagree, the manual wins.

Policy manual:
Company: Northwind Components

NW-LEAVE | Leave and absence | topic=leave
Rule: Annual leave must be requested in the HR system at least 5 working days ahead. Sick leave needs a message to the manager on the same day, and a doctor's note after 2 consecutive sick days. Leave is not taken during a published freeze week.
Required action when this policy applies: If the 5-day notice, the freeze week, or the sick-note rule was missed, do not approve the leave and tell the employee to file the correct request. If the request meets the rule, approve it and record the dates.

NW-EXPENSE | Expenses and reimbursement | topic=expenses
Rule: A business expense needs an itemized receipt and manager approval before it is reimbursed. Alcohol, personal travel, and gifts over 25 USD are not reimbursable. A claim must be filed within 30 days of the spend.
Required action when this policy applies: Reject a claim that is missing a receipt, missing prior approval, outside the allowed categories, or older than 30 days, and name the rule that failed. Approve a claim that has a receipt, prior approval, and an allowed category.

NW-GIFT | Gifts and conflicts of interest | topic=gifts
Rule: An employee may keep a gift or meal of 25 USD or less from a supplier. Anything above 25 USD is refused or handed to Compliance. A gift from a supplier during an open bid is refused at any value. A financial interest in a supplier is disclosed before any purchasing decision.
Required action when this policy applies: Require refusal or handover when the value cap or the open-bid rule is broken, and record a required disclosure. If the gift is 25 USD or less and no bid is open, record that it is allowed.

NW-PRIVACY | Customer data privacy | topic=privacy
Rule: Customer name, email, phone, and account id are used only for the stated service purpose, and only in an approved system. They are not copied to personal email, personal chat, or an unapproved tool. A suspected leak is reported to Privacy within 4 hours.
Required action when this policy applies: Stop an unapproved copy, tell the employee to delete it, and file a Privacy report if the data already left an approved system. If the use matches the stated purpose on an approved system, record that no breach occurred.

NW-SAFETY | Workplace safety | topic=safety
Rule: A machine guard is in place before the machine runs. A spill or an injury is reported to the shift lead immediately. An employee does not bypass a lockout tag.
Required action when this policy applies: Stop the job until the guard or the lockout is restored, and file an incident report for a spill or an injury. If the guard is in place and there is no incident, no safety report is required.

NW-REMOTE | Remote-work equipment | topic=remote
Rule: A company laptop stays company property and is not shared with household members. A lost or stolen laptop is reported to IT within 1 working day. Personal software is not installed without IT approval.
Required action when this policy applies: If the laptop was shared, lost, stolen, or given unapproved software, tell the employee to stop, report it to IT the same day, and return or reimage the device. Approved use of the company laptop needs no extra action.

NW-REPORT | Misconduct reporting | topic=reporting
Rule: Theft, harassment, falsified records, and retaliation are reported to the ethics line or to Compliance. A manager does not investigate a complaint about themselves. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a breach.
Required action when this policy applies: Direct the employee to the ethics line. If the manager is the subject, remove that manager from the investigation. Do not tell the employee to raise it only with the person they are accusing.

NW-ACCEPT | Acceptable use of company systems | topic=acceptable_use
Rule: Company systems are for business use. They are not used for outside paid work, gambling, or pirated software. A personal message of a few minutes is tolerated. Passwords are not shared.
Required action when this policy applies: If the use is outside paid work, gambling, pirated software, or password sharing, revoke the session and refer the employee to their manager. A brief personal message is recorded as allowed.

NONE: no policy in this manual applies. Required action: tell the employee the manual does not cover this and to ask the compliance officer. Do not invent a rule.
